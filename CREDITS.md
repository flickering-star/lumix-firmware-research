# Credits

The GM1 LUT Opacity blend cache is by **ShawnMayer**:
[gm1-lut-opacity-cache](https://github.com/ShawnMayer/gm1-lut-opacity-cache),
commit `52b4b23e93446d36e2024f3b4f59d39724aa55b6`.

Their209-byte add-on caches unchanged blended tables, restoring practical GM1
Opacity adjustment. It is integrated into the GM1 LUT option in updater0.2.14.
The original add-on assembly and recipe are retained in
[research/gm1-luts/opacity-cache](research/gm1-luts/opacity-cache).
The GM5 experiment is excluded; its released LUT implementation is unchanged.
